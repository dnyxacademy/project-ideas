Bring your own idea — the best projects usually are. Fill this in before you write any code.

If someone is mentoring you, get it reviewed first. On the Dnyx Academy programme that means a
mentor's approval by **Tuesday of Week 1** — post it in your batch chat or your lesson Q&A.

Keep it to one page. If you cannot describe it in one page, it is too big.

## What a reviewer is checking

- **Is it finishable?** Starting from close to zero, in the time you actually have.
- **Does it reach the bar?** Three related tables and a testable flow, or a dataset plus documents
  plus three tools.
- **Is the data real and allowed?** No scraped personal data, no dataset you cannot access, no
  "I will find the documents later".
- **Is it safe and legal?** Nothing handling real payments, real medical records, real personal data
  about people who have not agreed, or anything that needs a licence to operate.
- **Is it yours?** Rebuilding something as practice is fine. Cloning a tutorial you can follow
  along with is not — there is nothing left to learn.

A reviewer may ask you to cut it down. That is not a rejection; it is the most useful thing they
can do for you at the start.
